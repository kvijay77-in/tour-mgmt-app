# Rider float

_Last updated: 2026-09-29_

A ride may offer transport from Start to a configured later stage. Its float option and destination are ride-specific. A rider marked Float has not yet completed a riding-stage check-in: show a Float badge, but leave their grid cell in the not-checked-in color until they check in at the destination. At Start, Float, regular check-in and Not Riding are mutually exclusive; switching between them uses the [online Override or Cancel flow](check-in.md). Only offer float targets configured for that ride; onward stage check-ins then follow normal progress rules.

Where several floats exist, label them by destination option (Float 1, Float 2). A Float 2 rider does not appear as a missed check-in at the intermediate stage. Keep the badge visible through the destination-stage view without obscuring the rider ID; after the destination check-in, later stages follow ordinary progress. A float is always from Start to a later stage, never between intermediate stages.
